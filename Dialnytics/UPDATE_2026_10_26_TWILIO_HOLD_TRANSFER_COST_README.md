# Twilio Hold + Transfer, cost by country (2026-10-26)

Twilio lines (Twilio 1 and Twilio 2):
- Hold: the caller hears hold music, you stop hearing them; Resume brings both back. Done in the browser.
- Transfer: to a teammate on the same Twilio account (company agents) or to a phone number.
  Blind transfer: the caller is moved at once and your side ends. If the teammate does not
  answer in 25 s the caller can leave them a voicemail. Country / emergency rules apply to numbers.
- Add Call / Conference on Twilio lines: not yet.

Estimated cost:
- Rates per provider, direction and country zone: US/Canada, UK landline, UK mobile, other countries.
  Outgoing = country of the dialed number, incoming = country of your own number.
- Twilio calls also add the browser (Client) leg rate.
- Settings → Provider Rates shows the full grid. Old Telnyx/Twilio rates are kept as the US/Canada row.
- Defaults are estimates; set them from your Telnyx / Twilio price lists.
- No database changes.
